Data, uncertainty, and decisions.

I am Zhenyu Li, a PhD candidate in Operations Management at Nanyang Business School, Nanyang Technological University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Qinshen Tang.

My research is driven by an interest in how to better connect data, uncertainty, and action in decision-making. The causal revolution has pushed data-driven research beyond prediction, deepening our understanding of how interventions shape outcomes. But understanding the world is not the same as deciding what to do in it. This motivates my interest in prescriptive analytics, with my current research centered on robust optimization and project management.
