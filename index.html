I am Zhenyu (Zane) Li, a PhD Candidate in Operations Management at Nanyang Business School, Nanyang Technological University. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Qinshen Tang.

My research develops decision-focused models that connect data, uncertainty, and action. I am particularly interested in robust optimization for project planning, prescriptive analytics for operations, and decision-making when causal structure is uncertain.
